```typescript
  type: "review-bundle",
  id: "<id>",

    "show",
    "update",
    "destroy",
    "edit-transcripts",
    "view-stats",
    "share",
    "translate",
    "create-folders",
    "create-channels",
    "create-webinars",
    "manage-team",
    "edit",
    "order-audio-descriptions",
    "create-transcripts",
    "view-speakers",
    "view-tags",
    "manage-tags",
    "manage-allowed-domains",
  ],
};
```

The permissions granted on the object, by type:<br/><br/>- `media`: `show`, `update`, `destroy`, `edit-transcripts`, `view-stats`,<br/>  `edit`, `share`, `translate`, `order-audio-descriptions`, `create-transcripts`.<br/>  `edit` allows changing the media's player customizations. `edit-transcripts`<br/>  also lists and shows the media's localizations, and deletes them together<br/>  with `update`; no other permission reaches a media's localizations.<br/>  `translate` orders localizations (dubs). Any permission lists and shows the<br/>  media's extended audio descriptions, `update` deletes them and<br/>  `order-audio-descriptions` orders one. `edit-transcripts` is the only<br/>  permission that also reaches the media's captions and speaker data; `show`,<br/>  `update` and `destroy` apply to the media itself and never to those nested<br/>  resources. `create-transcripts` allows uploading and purchasing captions for<br/>  the media without reading or editing existing ones. `share` allows adding<br/>  the media to a new review bundle.<br/>- `folder`: `show`, `update`, `destroy`, `share`, `view-stats`. A rule also covers the<br/>  folder's subfolders: any permission lists and shows them, and `update`<br/>  creates, renames and deletes them. It also covers the folder's sharings:<br/>  any permission lists and shows them, `share` creates them, and `update`<br/>  edits and deletes them.<br/>- `account`: `create-folders`, `create-channels`, `create-webinars`,<br/>  `view-stats`, `manage-team`, `view-speakers`, `view-tags`, `manage-tags`,<br/>  `manage-allowed-domains`. `manage-team` allows inviting contacts to the<br/>  account. `view-speakers` lists the account's speaker profiles. `view-tags`<br/>  lists the account's tags, and `manage-tags` also creates and deletes them.<br/>  `manage-allowed-domains` lists, shows, adds and deletes the account's allowed<br/>  domains; without it a token sees none.<br/>- `webinar`: `show`, `update`, `destroy`, `view-stats`. A rule also covers<br/>  the webinar's registrations and collaborators: any permission lists its<br/>  registrations, and `update` registers attendees and lists, invites and<br/>  removes collaborators.<br/>- `channel`: `show`, `update`, `destroy`. A rule also covers the channel's<br/>  episodes and collaborators: any permission lists and shows its episodes,<br/>  and `update` adds, edits, publishes, unpublishes and removes episodes and<br/>  lists, invites and removes collaborators.<br/>- `review-bundle`: `show`, `destroy`. Creating a review bundle needs no<br/>  rule on a review bundle; it needs `share` on every media in it.<br/> |
